## Design Specifications

### Page Format:
- **Size:** A4 (210mm x 297mm)
- **Orientation:** Portrait
- **Margins:** 15mm all sides
- **Bleed:** 3mm (for professional printing)

### Color Palette:
- **Primary:** Navy Blue (#1e3a5f)
- **Secondary:** Gold (#d4af37)
- **Text:** Dark Grey (#333333)
- **Background:** White with navy/gold accents

### Typography:
- **Headings:** Montserrat Bold or similar (navy blue)
- **Subheadings:** Montserrat SemiBold (navy blue or gold)
- **Body Text:** Open Sans Regular (dark grey)
- **Sizes:** 
  - Page titles: 24-28pt
  - Section headings: 16-18pt
  - Body text: 10-12pt

### Images:
- High resolution (300 DPI for print)
- Professional photo treatment
- Consistent sizing and alignment
- Navy blue borders or frames as appropriate

### Branding:
- Logo on every page (bottom right corner or header)
- Consistent navy/gold color scheme throughout
- Page numbers on bottom center

---

## Production Notes

**For Print:**
- Professional printing service recommended
- High-quality paper stock (minimum 120gsm for pages, 250gsm for cover)
- Optional: Matte or gloss lamination for cover
- Binding: Perfect bound or saddle-stitched

**For Digital:**
- Export as optimized PDF
- Embed all fonts
- Include hyperlinks (email, website, social media)
- File size optimization for email distribution
