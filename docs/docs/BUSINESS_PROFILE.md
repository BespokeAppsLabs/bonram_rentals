# BONRAM RENTALS - Business Profile

## Company Overview

**BONRAM RENTALS** is a South African-based events and plant hire company specialising in reliable, high-quality rental solutions for events, construction sites, government projects, and corporate functions. We pride ourselves on professionalism, punctual service delivery, and well-maintained equipment that meets industry standards.

With extensive experience in servicing both private and public sector clients, BONRAM RENTALS has built a strong reputation for dependable service, hygiene-focused sanitation solutions, and flexible event support.
