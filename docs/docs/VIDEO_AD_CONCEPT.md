# BONRAM RENTALS - Video Ad Concept

**Purpose:** Short promotional video for social media, website, and presentations  
**Duration:** 60-90 seconds  
**Target Audience:** B2B, Government, Corporate, and B2C (weddings/events)  
**Tone:** Professional, trustworthy, aspirational  
**Style:** Cinematic, premium, South African pride

---

## Video Concept Overview

### Core Message:
**"Presidential-Grade Service. For Every Event."**

### Emotional Arc:

### Visual Style:
- Cinematic golden hour lighting
- Drone shots of South African landscapes

- No cheesy stock music—sophisticated, understated soundtrack

## Production Specifications

### Video Format:
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum, 4K preferred
- **Aspect Ratios:**  
  - 16:9 (YouTube, website, presentations)
  - 1:1 (Instagram feed)
  - 9:16 (Instagram/Facebook Stories, TikTok)
- **Frame Rate:** 24fps (cinematic) or 30fps (smooth)

### Audio:
- **Music:** Sophisticated, African-inspired instrumental
  - Examples: Marimba with modern production, orchestral with African drums
  - Should feel premium, not cheesy or over-the-top
  - Subtle, understated, professional
- **Voiceover (Optional):** Professional South African voice talent
  - Warm, authoritative, trustworthy tone
  - English with subtle South African accent
- **Sound Design:** Subtle ambient sounds (wind, nature)

### Color Grading:
- **Primary Tones:** Navy blue and gold
- **Mood:** Warm, golden hour lighting
- **Look:** Cinematic, slightly desaturated for sophistication
- **Contrast:** Medium-high for premium feel

## Production Options

### OPTION 1: Professional Production (Ideal)

**Requirements:**
- Hire professional videographer/production company
- Shoot on location with actual Bonram equipment
- Professional drone footage
- Multiple camera angles
- Professional color grading and editing

**Estimated Cost:** R25,000 - R50,000 (depending on crew size, equipment, locations)

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks (pre-production, shoot day, post-production)

---

### OPTION 2: Stock Footage + Actual Photos (Budget-Friendly)

**Requirements:**
- Use high-quality stock footage of South African landscapes
- Incorporate Bonram's actual equipment photos
- Professional editing to blend stock + photos seamlessly
- Motion graphics for text overlays
- Licensed music track

**Estimated Cost:** R5,000 - R15,000 (stock footage licenses + editing)

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks (sourcing footage, editing)

**Stock Footage Sources:**
- Shutterstock, Pond5, Artgrid (South African landscape b-roll)
- Look for: Golden hour SA landscapes, event setups, professional venues

---

### OPTION 3: AI-Assisted Animation (Modern Approach)

**Requirements:**
- Use generated concept images as key frames
- Animate with motion graphics and parallax effects
- Professional text animations
- Sophisticated transitions
- Modern, clean aesthetic

**Estimated Cost:** R10,000 - R20,000 (animator/motion designer)

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks
